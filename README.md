# Superstore Sales Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

## Project Overview

This project analyzes Superstore sales data using Microsoft Power BI to identify sales trends, profitability patterns, customer behavior, product performance, and shipping insights.

The dashboard transforms raw sales data into interactive visual reports to support business analysis and data-driven decision-making.

## Business Objectives

* Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
* Identify profitable and loss-making product categories.
* Understand customer segments and purchasing patterns.
* Compare performance across regions and states.
* Evaluate shipping methods and delivery timelines.
* Track key performance indicators (KPIs) and year-over-year growth.

## Tools and Technologies

* **Power BI Desktop:** Dashboard development and visualization
* **Power Query:** Data cleaning and transformation
* **DAX:** Measures and business calculations
* **Data Modeling:** Date table and relationship management

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Sales Overview

Provides a summary of sales, profit, orders, profit margin, year-over-year performance, and monthly sales trends.

### 2. Profitability and Discount Analysis

Examines profit by category, region, year, sub-category, and discount band to identify profitability patterns.

### 3. Customer Analysis

Analyzes customer segments, top customers, regional sales, state-level performance, and customer trends.

### 4. Geography and Shipping Analysis

Compares regional sales and profitability, shipping methods, and average shipping duration.

### 5. Product Performance Analysis

Highlights top-performing products and compares product-level sales, profit, and sub-category performance.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

* Total Sales
* Total Profit
* Total Orders
* Total Customers
* Profit Margin %
* Average Discount %
* Average Order Value
* Sales and Profit Year-over-Year Growth
* Average Shipping Days

## Key Features

* Year-based filtering
* KPI cards and comparative charts
* Regional and customer-level analysis
* Product performance analysis
* DAX-based calculations
* Date-based trend analysis
